Questions before the questions

Things people often ask.

How does the app work?

After purchase, you get instant access to the app in your browser — no install needed, works on phone, tablet, or laptop. Open it together, swipe through questions by chapter, save the ones you want to come back to. Sign in any time on any device.

Do I have to do exactly one a day?

Absolutely not. Some couples open it together every morning with coffee. Others save it for weekly date nights. Some pull a random question when things feel stuck. The structure is a gift, not a rule.

Is this for new couples or long-term partners?

Both. The app opens with lighter, getting-to-know-you questions and gradually moves into deeper territory. New couples build a foundation; long-term partners rediscover each other. Same questions, different journeys.

Do we both need to buy it?

Nope — one purchase, two people. Open it on a shared device, or sign in from each of your phones. The whole point is doing it together.

Would this work as a gift?

It's one of the most-given gifts in this category — for anniversaries, engagements, wedding showers, and Valentine's Day. You can send access directly to someone with a personal note.
